---

Evangelion Research is independent and deliberately small. We work in the open: notes are
published as drafts, code is released with the claims it supports, and negative results are
posted rather than buried.

The method is unglamorous. Take an interpretability claim that is currently stated informally,
find the formal apparatus that would make it precise, and then check whether the precise
version is still true. Often it is not, which is the point.
