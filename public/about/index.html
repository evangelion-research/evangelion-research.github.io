About Repurtum Research Lab.

Repurtum Research Lab is a small independent software research practice focused on interpreters, developer tools, and agent-assisted feedback loops.

The lab publishes drafts as research notes. A useful note should name the mechanism under study, describe the implementation clearly enough to reproduce, and leave behind questions that can be tested in the next iteration.
